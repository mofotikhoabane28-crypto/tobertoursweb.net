About Lesotho

A mountain kingdom completely surrounded by South Africa.

Our Story

Lesotho is home to the Basotho people, known for their woven hats, colourful blankets and warm hospitality. The country is the only independent state in the world entirely above 1,000 metres.

Why visit?

  • Dramatic mountain scenery
  • Living Basotho culture
  • Pony trekking and hiking
  • Winter skiing at Afriski
